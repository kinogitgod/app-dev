# My Favorite Rom-Com Movies

A list of my all-time favorite romantic comedies and why I love them.

---

## 🎬 Top Picks

| Movie Title | Release Year | Main Cast |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Notting Hill** | 1999 | Julia Roberts, Hugh Grant |
| **How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days** | 2003 | Kate Hudson, Matthew McConaughey |
| **10 Things I Hate About You** | 1999 | Heath Ledger, Julia Stiles |

---

### 💖 Iconic Quotes

> *"I'm also just a girl, standing in front of a boy, asking him to love her."*  
> — **Notting Hill**

> *"You can't lose a guy in 10 days if you never had him."*  
> — **How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days**

> *"I hate the way I don't hate you. Not even a little bit, not even at all."*  
> — **10 Things I Hate About You**

---

### Key Highlights
* **Notting Hill**: Charming British humor combined with an iconic soundtrack.
* **How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days**: Unmatched chemistry and hilarious competitive antics.
* **10 Things I Hate About You**: A classic modern adaptation of Shakespeare's *The Taming of the Shrew*.
